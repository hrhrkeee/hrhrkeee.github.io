ChorusSignage

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026

This policy explains how ChorusSignage handles data when you use the iOS and iPadOS app.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

Scenarios, imported photos and videos, display settings, Bluetooth group settings, and language preferences are stored on your device. ChorusSignage does not require an account and does not upload this content to a server operated by us.

Deleting the app also deletes its on-device app data, except for files you previously exported and saved elsewhere.

2. Sharing Between Devices over Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth to find nearby devices and share scenarios, media, display settings, and playback timing. Required data is transferred locally between devices using the same group settings. This communication does not use a server operated by us or require an internet connection.

You can still create and play scenarios on one device without granting Bluetooth access.

3. Camera, Photos, and Videos

The camera is used only to scan a QR code for joining a Bluetooth group. QR code video is processed on your device and is not saved as an image or video or sent to a server operated by us.

Photos and videos you select are used to create, play, and export scenarios and to share them with nearby devices you choose. They are not sent to a server operated by us without your action.

4. Purchases

The app uses Apple StoreKit for in-app purchases and purchase restoration. Apple processes purchase-related data under Apple's privacy policy. ChorusSignage reads verified, non-revoked purchase entitlements to hide advertising.

Optional consumable Support Development products support the developer. They do not unlock features or remove advertising.

5. Advertising, Consent, and Identifiers

When you use the free version, the app uses Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform. Google may collect or process approximate location inferred from an IP address, app or device identifiers, advertising data, ad and app interactions, crash data, performance data, and other diagnostics for ad delivery, consent management, fraud prevention, analytics, and related purposes.

Where required, the app presents Google's consent form. When applicable, you can change your choices from Privacy Choices in the app. ChorusSignage does not load ads until the consent state permits ad requests.

ChorusSignage does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use the IDFA. It also disables the Google Mobile Ads publisher first-party ID. Ad availability and content can vary with your consent, region, and device settings.

6. Retention and Deletion

On-device data remains until you change or delete it, or uninstall the app. Advertising and purchase providers retain data according to their own policies.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children. Do not use the app where processing advertising or purchase data would require parental consent that has not been obtained.

8. Third-Party Policies

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob privacy information
  • Apple Privacy Policy

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the app, SDKs, legal requirements, or data practices change. The effective date shows the latest version date.

10. Contact

For questions about this policy, use the ChorusSignage contact form.
