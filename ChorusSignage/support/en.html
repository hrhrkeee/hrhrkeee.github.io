Getting Started

  1. Choose an included sample under Scenarios, or create a new scenario.
  2. Add photo, video, or solid-color scenes and set their duration and transitions.
  3. Open the player and press Play.

Synchronizing Multiple Devices

  1. Enable Bluetooth synchronization in Settings on every device.
  2. Use the same group ID and password on each device, or join from an invitation QR code.
  3. Check the connection on the Displays screen and choose Standard, Mosaic, or Relay.
  4. Wait for required media transfers to finish, then start playback.

Bluetooth synchronization does not require an internet connection or OS pairing. Keep the devices nearby and make sure Bluetooth is enabled.

If Devices Do Not Connect or Synchronize

  • Enable Bluetooth and ChorusSignage's Bluetooth permission on every device.
  • Confirm that the group ID and password match.
  • After changing settings, save them and try Reconnect.
  • Keep the app in the foreground and move devices closer while media is transferring.
  • If the issue remains, restart ChorusSignage or restart each device.

Purchases and Restoration

Open Purchase Options from Settings to view the one-time and monthly ad-removal products and optional development tips. If a previous ad-removal purchase is not reflected, choose Restore Purchases.

Consumable development tips do not add features or remove advertising.

Contact

If you still need help, use the contact form. Include the device model, OS version, ChorusSignage version, and steps that produced the issue.
