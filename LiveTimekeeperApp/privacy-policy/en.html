LiveTimekeeper

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 15, 2026

This policy explains how LiveTimekeeper handles data when you use the iOS and iPadOS app.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

Timer presets, display preferences, language, chime settings, Bluetooth settings, and other app settings are stored locally on your device. LiveTimekeeper does not require an account and does not intentionally collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, health data, or other profile information through a server operated by us.

2. Preset Sharing

You can choose to share timer presets using QR codes, files, or the iOS and iPadOS system share menu. Shared presets may include phase names, durations, colors, chime settings, and display settings that you enter or select. LiveTimekeeper does not upload these presets to a server operated by us. You control when and with whom a preset is shared.

3. Nearby-Device Bluetooth Synchronization

LiveTimekeeper uses Bluetooth to discover and communicate with nearby devices when you enable timer synchronization. Participating devices exchange timer presets, playback state, timing information, display options, and chime commands needed for synchronization. This communication occurs directly between nearby devices and is not sent to a server operated by us. Bluetooth synchronization does not require an internet connection.

4. Purchases

The app uses Apple StoreKit for in-app purchases, subscriptions, tips, and purchase restoration. Apple processes purchase-related data under Apple's privacy policy. LiveTimekeeper reads verified purchase entitlements to provide purchased benefits, such as removing advertising. Optional consumable tips support development and do not unlock features.

5. Advertising, Consent, and Tracking

The free version of the app uses Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform. Google may collect or process identifiers such as the IDFA, device information, ad interaction data, approximate location inferred from network information, diagnostics, and other data for advertising, consent management, fraud prevention, analytics, or related purposes.

Where required, the app presents Google's consent or privacy options form. In accordance with iOS App Tracking Transparency, you can allow or deny tracking. The availability and personalization of ads may vary according to your consent choices, device settings, and region.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Settings and presets stored by LiveTimekeeper remain on your device until you change or delete them, reset them where an option is available, or uninstall the app. Advertising and purchase providers retain data according to their own policies. You can manage tracking permission in iOS Settings and eligible advertising privacy choices through the app when the privacy options entry is available.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children. Do not use the app where collecting or processing advertising or purchase data would require parental consent that has not been obtained.

8. Third-Party Policies

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob privacy information
  • Apple Privacy Policy

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the app, SDKs, legal requirements, or data practices change. The effective date shows the latest version date.

10. Contact
