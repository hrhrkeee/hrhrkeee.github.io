DepthCapture

Privacy Policy

Effective date November 26, 2026

This policy explains how DepthCapture handles data when you use the iOS and iPadOS app.

1. On-Device Data and Capture

The app uses the camera and depth sensors to capture and preview images and depth data. Depending on your recording settings, the app may record device motion data. Images, depth data, point clouds, and other capture data are processed on your device. Capture data and app settings are stored on your device. The app does not upload capture data to a server operated by the developer.

2. Exporting and Sharing Capture Data

You can manage capture data in the Files app or export it from the app and share it using iOS and iPadOS sharing features. How shared data is handled depends on the recipient or service.

3. Nearby-Device Bluetooth Synchronization

When you enable synchronized capture, the app uses Bluetooth to discover and connect to nearby participating devices. The devices exchange information needed to coordinate capture commands and timing.

4. Purchases

The app uses Apple StoreKit for in-app purchases, subscriptions, and purchase restoration. Apple processes purchase-related data under its privacy policy. The app verifies purchase entitlements to provide purchased benefits, such as access to features or ad removal.

5. Advertising, Consent, and Usage Analytics

The app uses Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform to serve ads and manage consent. Google may collect or process app and device identifiers, device information, ad display and interaction data, approximate location inferred from network information, and diagnostic information for purposes such as serving and measuring ads and preventing fraud.

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand usage and improve the app. Google may process information such as screen views, feature use and operation outcomes, in-app purchase activity, and app or device identifiers for analytics.

Where applicable, the app presents Google's consent form or privacy options. You can change tracking permission in iOS or iPadOS Settings. You can also change your advertising choices from “Privacy options” in the app when that option is available.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete capture data in the app or through the Files app. Uninstalling the app removes data stored within the app from your device. Advertising, analytics, and purchase providers retain and delete data according to their own policies.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children. If parental consent is required for data to be handled, please obtain it before using the app.

8. Third-Party Policies

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob privacy information
  • Apple Privacy Policy

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the app, the services we use, legal requirements, or data practices change. The effective date indicates when this version takes effect.

10. Contact

For questions about this policy or to request deletion of data, use the support page. We use the information and contact details you provide to respond and handle your request.
