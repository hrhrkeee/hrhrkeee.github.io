Getting Started

TacetCam lets you take photos and record videos from the same capture screen. Available capture settings and features may vary depending on your device, OS version, and selected camera.

Permissions

  • Camera access is required to take photos and record videos.
  • Microphone access is required when recording video with sound.
  • Add access to the photo library is required to save captured photos and videos.
  • Some capture aids, such as a level indicator, may use device motion data.

If you do not grant a permission, features that do not require that permission may still be available. You can change permissions in iOS Settings.

If Capture or Saving Does Not Work

  • Check that TacetCam has the permissions needed for the feature you are using.
  • Check that your device has enough free storage.
  • If the selected capture settings are not available on your device, try another setting.
  • If the issue remains, restart TacetCam or restart your device.

Advertising and Purchases

Advertising is shown during free use, and viewing an ad may be required depending on the applicable usage conditions. In-app purchases provide options to remove advertising.

If a previous purchase is not reflected, try Restore Purchases from the purchase screen. Optional development support products do not change app features or advertising conditions.

Contact

If you still need help, use the contact form. Include your device model, iOS version, TacetCam version, and the steps that produced the issue.
