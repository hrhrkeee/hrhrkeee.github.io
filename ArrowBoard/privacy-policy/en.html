Privacy Policy

English | 日本語

This policy explains how ArrowBoard handles data when you use the app on iPhone or iPad. The app does not require an account.

On-device data and Bluetooth sync

Arrow display settings, language preferences, and remaining reward time are stored on your device. When you use Bluetooth sync, nearby participating devices exchange the arrow's display state and information needed to connect and stay in sync. The app does not send this information to a server operated by the developer. You can display an arrow on one device without granting Bluetooth access.

Advertising and usage analytics

The app uses Google AdMob services to serve ads and manage consent, and Google Analytics for Firebase to understand app usage and improve the app. Google may process device or app identifiers, IP addresses and the approximate location inferred from them, screen views and interactions, ad activity, and diagnostic information for purposes such as serving and measuring ads, preventing fraud, and analyzing usage.

You can change tracking permission in your iPhone or iPad settings. You can change your advertising choices from the app's advertising privacy settings when that option is available.

In-app purchases and support

Apple processes in-app purchases, subscriptions, and purchase restoration. The app verifies purchase entitlements to provide purchased benefits. We use the information and contact details you provide when you contact us to respond and handle your request.

Retention, deletion, and third-party services

Deleting the app removes its locally stored data from your device. Google and Apple retain and delete the data they process under their own policies. We use third-party services that provide privacy protections consistent with this policy. We keep support information for as long as needed to handle your request. For questions about data handling or to request deletion, use the support form.
