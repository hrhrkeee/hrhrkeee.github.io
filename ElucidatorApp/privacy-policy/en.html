Elucidator

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how the iOS app Elucidator handles data.

1. On-Device Processing

Elucidator analyzes camera images with an AI model and describes the scene in text or speech. By default, it uses the on-device model provided through Apple’s Foundation Models framework. Unless you enable external integrations or display content on an external display, the app does not send images, your instructions (prompts), or analysis results outside your device. This content is not provided to advertising SDKs.

On-device processing refers to image analysis. Advertising, purchases, contact forms, and system-managed downloads or updates of models and related resources may require an internet connection.

External integrations (GET API, external control API, curl notifications, and external AI) are off by default. When enabled, they share analysis results, images, prompts, timestamps, and device status over your local network or send them to an endpoint you specify, depending on your settings. While the app is active in the foreground, the external control API accepts requests from devices on the same network to start or stop analysis or change the prompt. Turning off each feature stops sharing, sending, or accepting external control requests through that feature. Please check with the recipient’s administrator about how received data is used, retained, and deleted.

The app asks you to confirm data transmission when enabling external AI. Analysis with an external model sends images, prompts, system instructions, and related information to the API endpoint you specify. If you configure an API key, it is sent to that endpoint for authentication. Fetching the model list also uses the network, and connection tests send a fixed test image and test prompts rather than camera images. Switching to an on-device model or turning off external AI stops sending data to external AI for analysis.

2. Camera and Speech Output

The app uses the camera to display and analyze images. You can change camera permission in iOS Settings. Analysis results are read aloud using iOS text-to-speech features. The app does not record ambient audio through the microphone for this feature.

3. Analysis History

To display recent analysis history, the app temporarily keeps result text, a reduced-size copy of the analyzed image, the capture time, and the prompt used on your device. History is limited to recent entries and is not saved for restoration after the app restarts. The app does not save history images to your photo library.

Depending on your display settings, analysis text and images used for analysis are shown on an external display. This uses the same temporary history, and clearing history in the app also clears the history shown on the external display.

4. Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores camera, display, language, and speech settings; prompts and prompt history; the prompt for the external control API; external AI settings such as endpoint URLs, models, and display names; usage counts; reward expiration times; and similar information on your device. This data is used to restore settings, reuse prompts, support external integrations, and manage usage limits and feature access. Stored prompts are handled separately from the analysis history described in Section 3. Commands for curl notifications may contain authentication information and are stored in the Keychain on your device. External AI API keys and the authentication token shared by the GET API and external control API are also stored in the Keychain, restricted to this device and excluded from synchronization.

Prompts and camera images may contain personal information that you enter or capture.

5. Purchases

The app uses Apple StoreKit for in-app purchases and purchase restoration. Apple processes purchase-related data under its privacy policy. Elucidator checks verified purchase status to determine access to purchased features and ad removal.

6. Advertising, Consent, and Tracking

The app uses Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform. Google may collect or process identifiers such as IDFA, device and app information, ad display and interaction data, IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, diagnostics, performance data, and related information for advertising, consent management, fraud prevention, analytics, and related purposes.

Where required, the app presents Google’s consent forms or privacy options. You can change applicable consent settings in the app. Advertising-related data is handled under Google’s policies and your choices.

The app requests tracking permission through iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) for ad personalization and measurement. Google Mobile Ads SDK uses the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you grant permission. The app does not use IDFA without that permission. You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings. Ads may still be displayed if you do not allow tracking, depending on your consent choices, device settings, and other applicable conditions.

7. Other Data Practices

The app does not require account registration and does not access device location services, contacts, calendars, or health data. Information you send when contacting us is handled as described in Section 12.

Data handling by operating system features, such as iOS diagnostic data sharing, is governed by your device settings and Apple’s policies.

8. Retention and Deletion

You can clear analysis history in the app. Older entries are removed when the history limit is reached, and history is not restored after the app restarts. You can delete settings and prompts by deleting the app. You can delete commands for curl notifications stored in the Keychain by resetting settings. Resetting settings does not delete all stored prompts and prompt history.

Turning off external AI keeps its connection settings and API keys. Deleting a registered external model also deletes its API key. Resetting settings deletes registered external AI settings and API keys, as well as the prompt for the external control API. Regenerating the authentication token for the GET API and external control API invalidates the previous token. Deleting data on your device does not delete data already sent to a recipient.

Depending on your device settings, stored app data may be included in iCloud or other backups. This is not a cloud-sync feature provided by Elucidator. You can manage and delete backups through the relevant service’s settings. Advertising and purchase providers retain data under their own policies.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children. Do not use the app without parental consent where that consent is required for processing advertising or purchase data.

10. Third-Party Policies

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob privacy information
  • Apple Privacy Policy

For endpoints you specify, including external AI services, also check the provider’s or administrator’s privacy policy and terms regarding use of submitted data for training, retention periods, deletion methods, and related practices.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when the app, services we use, legal requirements, or data practices change. The effective date identifies when each version of this policy takes effect.

12. Contact

For questions about this policy or requests to delete information you have sent us, please use the contact form.

Contact details, messages, and other information you submit through the form are sent to us through Google’s services. We use and retain this information as needed to respond to inquiries, investigate reported issues, and provide related support. Please do not send personal information that is unnecessary for your request.
